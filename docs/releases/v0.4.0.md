# Command Table v0.4.0

## Before you update

**Make a verified backup first.** This release moves the server's SQLite database from schema 2 to schema 4. The migration runs by itself in a transaction and keeps every room, seat and game, but an older image refuses the newer database, so going back to v0.3.x needs the backup you made before updating. See [back up](https://github.com/Addison16/command-table/blob/v0.4.0/docs/deployment.md#back-up).

Keep the same Compose project, `.env`, data volume and public origin, and never use `down -v`. Browser clients can tap **Save & update** when prompted. Pin `0.4.0` or an image digest when a fixed version is preferred.

## What changed since v0.3.2.1

### New ways to play

- **Two-Headed Giant:** a preset for teams of two with one shared life total (30), one poison total (15 to lose) and one turn per team. Start it from Home with **Two-Headed Giant · 2 vs 2**.
- **Archenemy scheme deck:** all 102 official schemes with art and rules text. The deck shuffles, cycles through every card, keeps ongoing schemes face up, and is shared in rooms.
- **Planechase:** one shared planar deck built from every printed plane and phenomenon, with the planar die and its rising cost. A chip on the board names the current plane.
- **Dungeons:** Lost Mine of Phandelver, Dungeon of the Mad Mage, Tomb of Annihilation and the Undercity, with each player's place kept and completed dungeons counted.
- **Optional trackers:** Energy, Experience, Rad, Tickets, The Ring, City's blessing or any counter you name, plus Monarch, Initiative and Day and night. They are off until the table switches them on.

### Around the table

- **Winner screen and elimination notices:** eliminating a player shows a short notice on every screen, and the last player standing gets a winner screen with **Rematch** and **End game**.
- **Playgroup stats:** record who won, then see games, wins and win rate per player and per commander, and average game length. Stats stay in the browser, can be corrected, exported, and imported from another device without double counting.
- **Saved players:** keep a regular's name, color, commanders and seat background, and fill a seat in one tap at setup or when joining a room. **Shuffle seating** deals a random seating order.
- **Turn clock and time on turns:** with turn tracking on, the active seat shows how long the turn has run, and the recap lists the time each player or side spent on their turns.
- **Life graph and life history:** the recap image draws every player's life across the game, and player details list that seat's life changes with the time into the game.
- **Storm count and mana pool:** a scratchpad for a big turn that clears itself when the turn passes.
- **Random opponent** and **Look up a card:** pick a target opponent with a saved result, and read any card's rules text and rulings by name or Scryfall link.
- **Deck links:** paste a public Archidekt link to bring in a deck's commander and artwork. Moxfield links are recognized, but Moxfield currently refuses automated readers, so the app asks for the commander name instead.
- **Seat backgrounds:** use any card's art, or a photo kept on your device, in place of commander art.

### Shared rooms

- **Spectator links:** the host can switch on a read-only watch link and QR for a TV or a friend. Spectators never take a seat and lose access when the host switches it off.
- **Scan a room code:** the join screen reads the host's QR with the camera where the browser allows it.
- **Redo:** after undoing your own action, **Redo** puts it back until anyone changes the table.
- **Group life change for hosts:** the host of a room can apply a board-wide drain in one step, and undo it.
- **Reliability:** bounded room traffic and receipt retention, steadier reconnects and saves, verified automatic daily backups, and private operator diagnostics.

### Look and feel

- **Simulated dice:** every die is thrown with rigid-body physics, bounces and settles by itself, then shows the saved result. Dice gather in the center and stay clear of the safe areas.
- **Ten coordinated looks**, with four new color themes, and **Set the mood** on Home to switch in one tap.
- **Artwork framing:** move each saved commander's crop on your own device.
- **Faster life changes:** a long hold on + or − moves by five and then by ten, and the whole hold is one Undo.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** on a computer for undo, next turn, utilities, the game menu, history, settings and opening a seat.
- **A tidier Home screen** game list, and a fix for tapping commander suggestions.

### Hosting

- **Unraid template:** add Command Table from its template so it shows its icon on the Docker page.
- The image is published to `ghcr.io/addison16/commandtable` and the `commanders-table` compatibility name.

## Compatibility

Existing local saves, guest memberships and exports are read as before. Games saved by this release carry new optional fields, and earlier versions validate saves strictly, so exports from v0.4.0 may not import into an older app. Card art, deck links, card lookups and rulings need a connection; rules text for schemes, planes and dungeons works offline.
